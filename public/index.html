The Garden

a living map of who you talk to
How this works
  • Each person is a plant. Talking to them waters it back to full health; going quiet lets it wilt.
  • Friendship level (1–5) sets how the plant looks (from a simple sprout up to a full rose) — and the closer the level, the faster that plant wilts without contact, since you'd naturally reach out more.
  • + Add Plant anytime — name, tags, location and contact info are all optional, and typing a new tag or location creates its own bed automatically.
  • Log a conversation from a plant's detail view as many times a day as you talk to people — it resets their health and stacks onto their history.
  • By Relationship / By Location up top switch how the garden is grouped; All always shows everyone in that view.
